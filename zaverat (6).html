The Process

Handcrafted Perfection

01

Design & Sketch

Every piece begins as a vision. Our designers sketch intricate patterns inspired by nature, architecture, and emotion, refining every curve before metal is ever touched.

02

Molding & Casting

Using the ancient lost-wax technique alongside modern 3D printing, we create molds that capture the finest details, ensuring structural integrity and artistic fidelity.

03

Setting & Polishing

Our master setters place each stone by hand under microscopes. Finally, the piece is polished to a mirror finish, ready to reflect the light of the world.

Our Legacy

"True luxury is not about excess, but about the absence of vulgarity."

Established in 2004 in the heart of Ahmedabad, Zaverat Jewellers began as a vision to bring unparalleled craftsmanship to discerning clients. We honor the rich heritage of Indian jewellery making while embracing contemporary elegance.

Today, every piece tells a story of tradition, purity, and passion. We do not mass produce. We create limited series and bespoke commissions for those who understand that jewellery is an extension of the soul.
